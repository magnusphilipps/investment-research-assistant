Feature goal:
Add a clear financial statements section that helps a user understand:
1. Whether the business is making money
2. What the company owns and owes
3. Where cash actually came from and went

Use the existing project structure and yfinance integration. Do not rebuild the application or add later features such as valuation, news, peer comparison, AI analysis, Streamlit or report generation.

Required outputs

A. Income statement
Show the latest available annual figures, plus enough historical data to calculate trends where possible:

- Revenue
- Revenue growth
- Whether revenue growth is accelerating or slowing
- Gross profit
- Gross margin
- Operating income
- Operating margin
- Net income
- EPS

Definitions:
- Gross margin = gross profit / revenue
- Operating margin = operating income / revenue
- Revenue growth = percentage change from the prior comparable annual period

For revenue growth acceleration:
- Compare the latest annual revenue growth rate with the previous annual revenue growth rate.
- Clearly label it as accelerating, slowing, broadly stable or unavailable.
- Do not make the conclusion if there is insufficient historical data.

EPS:
- Display reported or diluted EPS where available.
- Do not claim EPS growth is driven by buybacks unless share-count data is available and supports that conclusion.
- If possible, also display diluted average shares outstanding so the user can compare EPS growth with changes in share count.

B. Balance sheet
Show:

- Cash and cash equivalents
- Total debt
- Total shareholder equity
- Debt-to-equity ratio
- Current assets
- Current liabilities
- Current ratio
- Retained earnings

Definitions:
- Debt-to-equity ratio = total debt / total shareholder equity
- Current ratio = current assets / current liabilities

Handle negative or zero shareholder equity carefully:
- Do not calculate a misleading debt-to-equity ratio.
- Display “Not meaningful” with a brief reason where appropriate.

C. Cash flow statement
Show:

- Operating cash flow
- Capital expenditure
- Free cash flow

Definition:
- Free cash flow = operating cash flow - capital expenditure

Important:
- Some data sources report capital expenditure as a negative cash outflow.
- Normalise the sign correctly so free cash flow is not accidentally added or subtracted twice.
- Clearly document the approach in the code.

Presentation requirements

- Keep data retrieval, calculations and display formatting separated.
- Display values in the company’s reporting currency where that information is available.
- Format large figures clearly, for example:
  - £125.4B
  - $8.7M
- Format percentages consistently.
- Label annual periods clearly.
- Do not present missing values as zero.
- Use “N/A” where information is unavailable.
- Do not crash when a line item is missing or named differently.
- Preserve the existing invalid-ticker handling.
- Preserve all existing Feature 1 and Feature 2 functionality.

Data reliability requirements

- Use annual financial statements rather than quarterly figures for this feature.
- Retrieve at least three annual periods where available so growth and acceleration can be assessed.
- yfinance line-item labels may vary between companies, so create a robust helper that checks reasonable alternative names for each financial item.
- Do not silently substitute unrelated accounting fields.
- If a calculation cannot be made safely, return N/A and explain why in the display where useful.
- Add clear docstrings and comments around financial calculations.
